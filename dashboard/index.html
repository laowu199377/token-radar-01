How the model works

implied value = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity

Base — TVL × 0.25

A token is worth a quarter of the TVL it secures. The protocol's own locked value is the floor of the claim.

Growth — 7-day change

The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150% and weighted ×0.5. A week's spike never decides the ranking — the snapshot's fastest riser printed +4448%.

Maturity — no-token duration

0.50 at listing, rising to 1.20 after four years. A protocol that has run for years without a token is worth more than one that listed last month; the wait itself is the signal. No listing date on file counts as one year old.

What never gets ranked

Wrapped, staked and bridged products (WBTC, Binance staked ETH, USDT0…) and exchange products are left off this board: their token already trades, so they are not launch candidates. They appear under “Already trading” instead.

Implied value is this entry's claim, not a price target and not financial advice. It is computed from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot alone — TVL, 7-day change and listing date — with no protocol name hardcoded into the ranking.

How the farm plan is built. Each protocol gets 7 steps, built from its own figures: the capital tier comes from its TVL ($250 at ≥$1B, $150 at ≥$200M, $100 at ≥$50M, $50 below that); the hold length comes from its age (60 days if listed under 6 months, 45 under a year, 30 after that); the main and second actions are chosen by category — lending, DEX, curator, prediction, RWA, derivatives, stablecoin, or a default deposit. Every step states a dollar amount and a time cost.
